> Free, privacy-conscious commercial tools and fixed-scope human reviews for freelancers and small studios in India.

Scopewise helps independent professionals price projects, define scope, prepare proposals and statements of work, control revisions, invoice clients, and follow up on overdue payments. The core calculators and generators work without an account. Human help is optional and fit is confirmed by email before payment.
